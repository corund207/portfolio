Behind the systems

Builder, coder, designer.

I’m Jonah, a high school student who spends most of his free time building robots and the software behind them. I write the competition code for VEX V5 team 56S and handle most of the robot’s CAD.

I’m especially interested in the point where code has to deal with a messy physical system. That has led me into odometry, motion control, perception, simulation, and a lot of debugging tools.
